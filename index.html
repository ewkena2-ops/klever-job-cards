Cards per A4 sheet

Colour runs down the left edge for the salesperson and the right edge for the designer, so a card says whose job it is without being read. Every sheet is A4 paper — the four-card setting fits four A6 cards on one A4, and each of the four is set up on its own, so one sheet can carry four different salesperson and designer pairs. Print at 100% / actual size, then cut once across and once down.
